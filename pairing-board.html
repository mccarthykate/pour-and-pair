Pour & Pair

Learn the five ideas behind a good pairing, then pick a wine or a cheese from the Trader Joe's aisle, see what goes with it, and build the rest of the board.

The five ideas

Match intensity: big with big, delicate with delicate Contrast: acid or bubbles against cream, sweet against salt Share a flavor: nutty with nutty, earthy with earthy

Board-building tips

Serve reds slightly cool and whites not ice cold. Take cheese out of the fridge about 30 to 60 minutes ahead so the flavor opens up.
Aim for one soft, one firm and one bold cheese. Variety in texture matters as much as variety in flavor.
Taste in order from mild to strong, and sip water between pairings. Strong blue cheese first will flatten everything after it.
Cut the rind off before judging a pairing only if you dislike it. Bloomy rinds on brie are edible and add earthy notes that suit Pinot Noir.
